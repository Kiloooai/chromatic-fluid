# Neon Fluid Pendulum — Interactive Physics Toy

## What it is
A mesmerizing browser-based physics simulation where glowing neon orbs swing like pendulums from invisible pivots, leaving colorful trailing ribbons behind. Click and drag to fling orbs, attract them with your mouse, or create vortex swirls. Each orb has unique pendulum physics, and the trails paint the screen with fluid neon patterns.

## Build details
- Type: 3 (interactive browser toy)
- Repo: https://github.com/Kiloooai/neon-fluid-pendulum
- Status: ✅ Pushed and live

## How to play
• Click & drag anywhere to interact with orbs
• Choose attraction, repulsion, or vortex mode
• Watch the pendulum physics create organic motion
• Toggle pause and adjust gravity/damping
• Switch neon color schemes for different vibes

## Technical bits
• Pendulum-based physics with angle integration
• Trail systems with alpha-fading
• Multiple neon color schemes (cyberpunk, rainbow, sunset, matrix, arctic)
• Mouse interaction modes (attract, repel, vortex)
• 60fps canvas rendering with glow effects
• Fully responsive & touch-friendly

# Chromatic Fluid — Interactive Fluid Sim

## What it is
A browser-based fluid simulation where colored droplets flow, swirl, and merge. Mouse movement creates currents, clicks spawn droplets, and colors blend in real-time. Pure JavaScript, no frameworks, works offline.

## Build details
- Type: 3 (interactive browser toy)
- Repo: https://github.com/Kiloooai/chromatic-fluid
- Status: ✅ Pushed and live

## How to play
- Move mouse slowly to stir the fluid
- Click to spawn a droplet at cursor
- Double-click to create an explosion of droplets
- Watch colors merge and swirl

## Technical bits
- Particle-based fluid sim (~1k particles)
- Gravity, friction, mouse repulsion
- Additive color blending for glow
- Trail-based motion blur
- Auto-respawning particles

Built 2026-04-28 11:00 — because fluid simulations are soothing.

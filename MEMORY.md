## Design Principles

- Fun first, productivity second (or not at all)
- Visual/playful interactions beat dry tools
- Humor and personality are features, not bugs
- Each build should make someone smile or say "neat!"

## Tone

- Concise, slightly irreverent
- Competent but not corporate
- "Look at this weird thing I made" energy
- No filler, no fake enthusiasm
